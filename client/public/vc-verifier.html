VC Presentation — the debugger as the Verifier

Here the debugger is the Verifier of OpenID for Verifiable Presentations 1.0: it asks a wallet for claims and checks what comes back. You compose a DCQL query, the page signs the Authorization Request with a certificate it makes for this run, and you hand the request to a wallet. The wallet posts its answer to a response_uri this debugger's api hosts on the page's behalf, because a page cannot receive a POST. Every check on the answer is reported on its own line. The other side, the debugger as the Wallet, is the presentation workflow.
