Gordian Envelope — a credential you elide rather than re-sign

Gordian Envelope (IETF draft-mcnally-envelope-12, over dCBOR, draft-18) is a Merkle tree of digests: any claim can be elided — replaced by its digest — and every digest above it, so the issuer's signature over the root, stays the same. A holder presents by eliding; nothing is re-signed. Credentials are signed wrapped, so every claim is under the signature. Text form: ur:envelope/…. Held byte for byte to Blockchain Commons' Rust crates' own test vectors. Nothing on this page is stored.
