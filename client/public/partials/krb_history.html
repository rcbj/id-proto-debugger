Operations History

Every action this debugger has taken in the Kerberos workflow, newest first — from every page, not just this one. Call made names the exchange as the wire names it (AS-REQ, TGS-REQ, AP-REQ), so a row here lines up with a packet capture taken beside it.

Result is one of: Failure, the call never left the browser (a name that would not parse, no ticket held, a refused relay) or the far end refused it; Sent, it went out and nothing has come back — a row that stays Sent means no answer ever reached this debugger, so look at the KDC's own log rather than at this page; Success, the far end answered and the answer says the call worked. A KDC refusing a request it understood is a Failure here and is named by its KRB-ERROR code, because KDC_ERR_PREAUTH_REQUIRED and KDC_ERR_C_PRINCIPAL_UNKNOWN are entirely different problems and the status line above says so in prose.

The local actions that change what the workflow holds — making a stored ticket active, discarding one — are recorded too, and that is the point of interleaving them: a TGS-REQ that suddenly starts working is explained by the row above it. Kept in this browser's local storage, capped at 1,000, and it survives a reload. Unlike the Ticket History pane it holds no session keys, so it is not affected by the credential-cache checkbox.

The Decoder page shows this list and never adds to it. That page's one guarantee is that nothing you hand it persists — it is where a captured message gets pasted together with the long-term key that opens it — and this log lives in local storage, so recording even that a decode happened would break the promise for the sake of the least informative row in the workflow.
