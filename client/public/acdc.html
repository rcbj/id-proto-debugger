ACDC — a credential verified by its issuer's history

Authentic Chained Data Containers (Trust over IP KERI Suite specification) carry no signature: a credential's SAID — the digest of its own bytes — is sealed by an issuance event in the issuer's registry, and that event is anchored by a seal in an event of the issuer's KERI key event log, which is signed. Verifying one walks that chain, all of it in a CESR stream: the KELs (weighted thresholds, witness receipts, delegation), the registry (version 1 vcp/iss/rev, backer registries, version 2 rip/upd/bup), the credential (version 1, or version 2 and its most compact form), its schema (JSON Schema), its aggregate (selective disclosure), and the credentials its edges chain to. Both CESR versions are read and issued. Nothing on this page is stored.

Verify a Presentation
The issuer's KEL (and its delegator's, and the KELs of chained credentials' issuers), the registry events, the credential with its attachments, any credentials its edges point to, and optionally an IPEX grant (an exn) — concatenated as KERIA, keri-ts or this page sends them. Version 1 and version 2 messages may be mixed.
Optional: the JSON Schema the credential's s names, or a JSON list of schemas (one per credential in a chain). Each $id is checked to be its own SAID, and the credential, as presented, is validated against it (draft-07, or 2020-12 when its $schema says so).
Verify the last IPEX grant (an exn with route /ipex/grant) in the stream: its SAID, the sender's signature against the sender's KEL, its embeds' SAIDs, and the credential it carries.

  Issue a Credential
  

    
A fresh issuer each time: its inception, a registry, the credential and its issuance, each anchored by its own interaction event (exactly one seal each, which keri-ts requires). The page also runs the issuer's witnesses, backers and delegator, so it can sign their receipts and approvals.

  Disclose Selectively
  

    
From a credential issued above with "Aggregate": the same credential with only some of its blocks expanded and the rest replaced by their SAIDs. Its SAID does not change in version 2, so the registry still binds it.
